TextHop Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · Mildbit Studio

한국어

Mildbit Studio (“we”, “us”) provides TextHop (“the app”). This policy explains what information the app processes and what it does not.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not offer sign-up, login, or user accounts.
  • We do not send camera images or recognized text to any server.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or analytics SDKs.

2. Camera and Text Recognition

  • We request camera access solely to recognize text. No other permissions are requested.
  • Camera frames and recognition results are processed entirely on your device.
  • OCR runs on-device using Apple’s VisionKit and Vision frameworks.

3. Data Storage and Deletion

  • Collected text is kept only in memory while the app is running.
  • It is removed immediately when you quit the app or use “Delete Session Data” in Settings.
  • The app does not access your photo library or save images.
  • Settings such as the default mode and output format are stored in UserDefaults on your device and contain no personal information.

4. Clipboard and Universal Clipboard

  • Copied text is stored in the iOS clipboard.
  • If Handoff and Universal Clipboard are enabled on devices signed in with the same Apple ID, you can paste the text on a Mac or other device.
  • The lifetime and deletion of clipboard data are managed by iOS and Universal Clipboard. The app cannot control data remaining on other devices or confirm whether it was delivered.

5. External AI Services

  • The app does not integrate with external AI services such as ChatGPT or Claude.
  • When you paste copied text into an external service, that service’s privacy policy applies.
  • Exercise caution with sensitive or confidential information, and review the service’s policy and your organization’s security rules.

6. Third Parties

  • We do not collect or share your data with third parties.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children and does not collect children’s personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will announce changes on this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email support@mildbit.com.
